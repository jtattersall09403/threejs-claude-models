## Head

- **Silhouette**: crocodilian. Low, wide cranium; long broad snout roughly as long as the
  braincase. Heavy overhanging brow. Jaw closed, long mouth-line crease.
- **Horns**: ONE large pair, bone/cream, emerging above-behind the eyes, sweeping
  **back, up and slightly out**, ~0.9× head length, thick at base tapering to a point.
  A **dark banded ring** near the base (clearly visible in rear 3/4 + profile shots).
- **Crown spikes**: a fan/row of ~5 smaller cream conical spikes over the top-rear of
  the skull, pointing up-and-back, tallest near the centre.
- **Jaw / cheek spikes**: 3 small cream spikes per side along the lower jaw / behind the
  jaw hinge, pointing back-down. Extra small spikes at the mouth corner.
- **Eyes**: large, **amber/gold iris with a vertical slit pupil**, deep-set in a dark
  recessed socket. Dark blue-black scaled band wraps the socket and temple.
- **Head plating / colour**: near-black armoured plate over the top of the skull, with a
  **maroon / dark-red patch** over the brow ridges and between the eyes. Muzzle top is
  olive with a fine hexagonal pebble-scale pattern down the centre.
- **Throat / underside**: lighter yellow-olive with **distinct horizontal banded scutes**.

## Body

- Male humanoid, average build, slightly hunched shoulders. Reptilian tail (canonical
  Argonian; not visible in the reference crops but required for a full body).
- **Skin**: olive/moss green base, darker olive-brown mottling, fine pebbled scales.
  Dorsal surfaces darker, ventral lighter.
- **Hands**: bare green scaly, clawed.

## Clothing (Skyrim "Farm Clothes")

- Coarse brown/umber long-sleeved tunic over a lighter grey-green undershirt visible at
  the collar and centre chest.
- **Diagonal braided strap/sash**, the character's RIGHT shoulder → LEFT hip.
  (In the front-view screenshot it runs from upper-left of frame to lower-right; since
  we see the character from the front, that is their right shoulder to their left hip.)
- Wide cloth **belt** at the waist with a knotted hanging end at the front.
- Sleeves end mid-forearm with **pale cloth wrist wraps**; forearms and hands bare.
- Trousers/skirt below the belt, darker; worn leather shoes.
- Palette: desaturated umber, grey-brown, muted — nothing saturated.

## Lighting / mood (the "graphics quality" bar)

Dim warm candle-lit interior. Strong key from the front-left, deep shadows, warm rim
separating the silhouette from a near-black background. Low overall exposure, filmic
roll-off, heavily desaturated. Materials read as rough PBR: no plastic highlights.
